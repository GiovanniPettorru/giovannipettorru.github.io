Biography

Giovanni Pettorru is a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the University of Cagliari (DIEE), contributing to the Horizon Europe project "HEAT – Hybrid Extended reAliTy" with a focus on GPS-free localisation for immersive XR environments. His research spans IoT security, trustworthy Location-Based Services, and secure low-latency communications. He holds a Ph.D. in Electronic and Computer Engineering (2026, cum laude) and has authored over 20 peer-reviewed publications. A finalist in three IEEE thesis competitions (3MT/4MT), he earned two 3rd place awards. His projects AGROS, LENS, and GreenShield have received national recognition and a Silicon Valley acceleration through the SERICS Lab2Bay programme.

Giovanni Pettorru is currently a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (DIEE) of the University of Cagliari, contributing to the Horizon Europe project "HEAT – Hybrid Extended reAliTy" with a focus on GPS-free localisation for immersive XR environments. He earned his Ph.D. in Electronic and Computer Engineering in 2026 with the highest honours (cum laude), with a thesis titled Energy-Efficient and Trustworthy Location-Based Services for Next-Generation IoT, after completing his Master's Degree in Internet Engineering (cum laude, 2022) and his Bachelor's Degree in Computer Engineering (2020) at the same institution. During his doctorate, he spent a research period as a Visiting Ph.D. Student at the Instituto Politécnico de Viana do Castelo, Portugal.

His research interests span GPS-free localisation and trustworthy Location-Based Services, IoT security — including traffic classification and privacy-preserving communications — and secure low-latency protocols (HTTP/3, WebSocket, MQTT). He has authored over 20 peer-reviewed publications in top IEEE/MDPI venues, including IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management, IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications, and Future Generation Computer Systems. Beyond research, Dr. Pettorru is committed to education, having served as Teaching Assistant and Adjunct Professor for courses including Network Security, Cloud Networking, Digital Media, and Telecommunication Networks.

An active member of IEEE ComSoc, he has demonstrated outstanding science communication skills as a finalist in three prestigious thesis competitions: 3rd place at the Three-Minute Thesis (3MT) at IEEE ICC 2023, finalist at the Four-Minute Thesis (4MT) at IEEE GlobeCom 2024, and 3rd place at the 4MT at IEEE GlobeCom 2025. He serves as Web and Social Media Chair for the IEEE ComSoc Italy Chapter, is a member of the Organizing Committee for IEEE MeditCom 2026, and serves as Technical Programme Committee member for IEEE ICC 2027 (SAC—eHealth) and ICCE Berlin 2026. He also acts as peer reviewer for IEEE journals including IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology, IEEE Internet of Things Journal, and IEEE Communications Letters.

Dr. Pettorru's entrepreneurial vision has led to three award-winning projects: GreenShield, selected for the exclusive SERICS Lab2Bay acceleration in Silicon Valley at the INNOVIT hub, addressing resource-aware IoT security; LENS, a top-10 finalist at Start Cup Sardegna 2025 and recipient of the "Climate Change" special mention for its approach to detecting extreme natural events; and AGROS, recognised as one of the top-10 high-tech innovative ideas for the agri-food sector (AgriFuture 2025) and awarded second prize at Start Cup Sardegna 2024.
